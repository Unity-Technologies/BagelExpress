# Bagel Express — changelog

## Versioning

- **One source of truth:** Project Settings › Player › **Version** (`bundleVersion` in
  `ProjectSettings/ProjectSettings.asset`). It is read at runtime as `Application.version`.
- **Where it shows:**
  - In the game: `VersionLabel` on the title screen and next to the Settings title.
  - In the editor: the **Bagel UI** window's toolbar.
  - In the macOS player: its bundle version.
- **Format:** `MAJOR.MINOR`: MINOR for each demo-ready version, MAJOR for a big rework.
  Later, patch releases of a finished version will be `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`.
